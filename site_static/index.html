What triggers an alert

  • Three alert levels: WATCH (fast-growing Earth-facing sunspot), WARNING (X-flare potential above 10%, or an M5+ flare) and ALERT (X-class flare)
  • Earth-facing (full or partial) solar flares, labelled impulsive or long-duration, and filament eruptions
  • Earth-facing sunspot regions that look dangerous: a beta-gamma-delta magnetic field, rapid growth, a high X-class flare probability, rapid growth in a flare-prone McIntosh class, or M/X flares already produced; updates are posted when a flagged region meets further criteria
  • Coronal hole streams forecast to reach Earth; forecast G4+ storms; observed Kp at or above the threshold
  • Solar proton levels (≥10 MeV and ≥100 MeV) above their thresholds
  • Solar wind speed, density, total field (Bt) and southward Bz beyond their thresholds
